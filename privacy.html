Privacy Policy

LPG Backup · Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy describes the intended operation of LPG Backup, a privately operated utility for backing up a self-hosted LPG Management system to the owner's authorized Google Drive account. The utility is not offered as a public service.

1. Information accessed

With the Google account owner's explicit authorization, the backup utility may process Google account identification and OAuth authorization information needed to connect to Google Drive. It uploads, lists, downloads, and deletes backup files as needed for recovery and retention, within the permissions granted by Google.

The backup contents may include business records, application configuration, authentication data, and files from the LPG Management system. Those data are processed for backup and restoration rather than for advertising or profiling.

2. Purpose and use

Google Drive access is used only for creating, checking, retaining, and restoring backup data and for viewing storage/transfer status. The utility is not intended to browse unrelated personal Drive content or use Google user data for advertising.

3. Storage and security

Google OAuth credentials and tokens are intended to be held by the private backup server, not by this informational website. The system is designed to encrypt backup contents before uploading them to Google Drive. Access to backup administration is restricted to the system administrator.

No security method can guarantee absolute protection. Operational safeguards and encryption must be configured and verified before production use.

4. Sharing

Google Drive stores the backup files as the authorized storage provider. LPG Backup does not sell Google user data or intentionally share it with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Access may occur where necessary for legal obligations or through providers required to operate the owner's infrastructure.

5. Retention and deletion

Backup copies are kept according to the administrator's configured retention schedule and may be deleted when they expire. Authorization details may remain on the private server until the connection is removed. The Google account owner can revoke access from Google Account connections and may request deletion of application-held credentials.

Revoking OAuth authorization prevents future authorized access; it does not necessarily delete backup files already stored in Google Drive. Those files can be managed in Drive or through the backup administration process.

6. This website

These informational pages do not include analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, or Google sign-in functionality. The site host may process routine connection logs under its own policies.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the backup utility. Questions or requests about the utility's Google Drive access may be sent to siamsifat244@gmail.com.
