Private backup utility

Reliable recovery for LPG Management

LPG Backup is a privately operated utility intended to save recovery copies of a self-hosted LPG Management system to a Google Drive account authorized by its owner. It is not a public file-storage or backup service.

What it does

  • Creates scheduled recovery copies of the application's PostgreSQL database and necessary system files.
  • Uses Google Drive, with the account owner's consent, as off-site backup storage.
  • Supports retention and restoration of backup copies in case of server failure or data loss.

Google Drive access

The owner explicitly authorizes a Google account through Google's OAuth consent process. Access is used to transfer and manage backup files within the permissions granted. The utility is not intended to read unrelated personal documents or offer access to other users.

The permissions requested during authorization are the authoritative description of what the connected Google account permits.

Private operation and security

The backup process is intended to run on a private server with server-side credentials. Backup contents are intended to be encrypted before off-site storage. The website itself is informational and does not connect to Google Drive or collect sign-in credentials.

See the Privacy Policy for further information about access, storage, retention and revocation.
